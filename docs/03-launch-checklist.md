## Before texting registration (A2P), so this week
- [ ] File an Arkansas LLC (~$45 online) and get a free EIN from irs.gov. The builder recommends registering the Returnline brand once as a business (Standard) rather than as a sole proprietor, since a sole-proprietor registration reportedly can't be converted later [VERIFY IN GHL]
- [ ] A free one-page site inside GoHighLevel with a privacy policy and texting terms. Registration reviewers commonly reject campaigns without one

## Before the first trial goes live (the day a shop says yes)
- [ ] Open a free business checking account and keep business money separate
- [ ] Have a lawyer review the trial agreement, guarantee and texting-consent wording (1 hour)
- [ ] Sign up for GoHighLevel and build the demo using `product/mvp-demo-spec.md`
- [ ] Start the business-texting registration (A2P 10DLC) through GoHighLevel the same day. It can take days to weeks
- [ ] agent-designer builds the texting AI (request mode) and safety-qa tests it

## Before the first invoice
- [ ] Payments through GoHighLevel's built-in billing, or Stripe
- [ ] Set aside 25% of every payment for taxes
