# After-hours test calls

**Goal:** find out which shops don't pick up when they're closed. This is not a sales call; you're only checking.

## Before you call
- Call from a number you're fine with them calling back. A free Google Voice number keeps your personal cell private.
- Best times: weeknights after 7pm and weekends. Sunday night counts.
- Have this table open. Fill in one line per call.

## Log each call
| Shop | Day and time | Result | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| | | Rang out / Voicemail / Answering service / Person answered / Disconnected | Did the voicemail promise a callback? Did they call back, and when? |

## If nobody answers
Hang up. Don't leave a fake message. "Voicemail" or "rang out" is the proof we need.

## If a person or answering service picks up
Ask something honest and short: *"Hi, what are your hours tomorrow?"* Then say thanks and hang up. Don't make up a broken AC; it wastes their time and the owner may be the one who answers.

## If they call you back later
That's a warm opener, so be straight with them:
*"Hey, thanks for calling back! I'm Jordan, I'm local in Conway. I called after hours to see who picks up. I'm working on something that texts customers back when HVAC shops miss calls. Could I stop by this week and show you in five minutes?"*

## After the calls
Text Claude the results as a quick list, like "Mathis: voicemail, 8:05pm Sun". Claude logs each shop on the HQ dashboard and ranks your walk-ins, putting shops that missed calls first.
