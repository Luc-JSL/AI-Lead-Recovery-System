## Research method / caveats
- Direct WebFetch of company pages, BBB pages, and directories was not attempted this session; all findings come from **WebSearch result snippets and AI-synthesized search summaries**, which sometimes blend multiple sources without a clean single citation (flagged inline above wherever that happened).
- Where a search result offered a name that looked like it was inferred from the company name or street address rather than stated as fact (e.g., "Mr. Henigan" for All Heating and Air, which sits on Henigan Rd; "Austin" for Sub Zero with no surname), it was **not** used as a confirmed name, per the no-guessing rule.
- Title inconsistencies were kept visible rather than resolved by guessing (e.g., Todd Mathis is called both "Vice President" and "Owner" across sources; Kirby's has both a "CEO" and an "Owner" listed).
- No personal cell numbers, home addresses, or personal (non-business) social profiles are included anywhere in this file.
- Before any call, confirm the phone number is still active and ask "Is [name] still with the company / still the person who handles this?" as a safety check, especially for the Medium-confidence rows.
