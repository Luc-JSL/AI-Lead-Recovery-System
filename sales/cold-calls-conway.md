# Cold calls: the 5 Conway shops you tested

Every call has one goal: **book a 10-minute visit.** You aren't selling on the phone.
Your test calls were at **1:45am Monday**. Say "early Monday morning" (or "early this morning" if it's still Monday).

**Visit times to offer:** Wed 12:15-2:30 or Fri 12:15-3:30 (Mon 1-4 if you're calling early in the week).
**Every shop:** if the owner is out, ask the front desk: *"When's the best time to catch him, and is there a better number?"* Then log it.

## 1. Mathis Heating & Cooling · (501) 329-5874
**Ask for:** Todd Mathis. He runs the day-to-day; the family has run the business since 1965.
**Fit:** ⭐ Strong. They advertise 24-hour emergency service, but a night caller gets voicemail and a callback the next morning.

**Front desk**
> "Hi, this is Jordan with Returnline, I'm local here in Conway. Is Todd around?"
> *If they ask what it's about:* "It's about the calls that come in after hours. I called Monday morning and got your voicemail, and I had a quick idea for him. It takes 2 minutes."

**Todd**
> "Hi Todd, this is Jordan with Returnline. I'm local here in Conway. I know I'm calling out of the blue. Can I have 30 seconds, and then you tell me if it's worth talking?"
>
> *(He says sure.)*
>
> "I called your line early Monday morning. The voicemail said you'd call people back in the morning in the order they called. Honestly, that's a better setup than a lot of shops have. My question is: by the time you call back, how many of those people have already booked someone else?"
>
> *(Let him answer. Don't fill the silence.)*
>
> "That's what I work on. When a call goes unanswered, the caller gets a text from Mathis within a minute, even at 9 at night, asking what's wrong, the address and how urgent it is. The job request is waiting for you in the morning, and nobody's shopping around."
>
> "Could I stop by for 10 minutes and show you? Would Wednesday around 1 or Friday around 12:30 be better?"

**Likely pushback**
- *"We have 24-hour emergency service already."* → "Totally. Is that a person who answers at night, or the voicemail I got? If someone calls at 9pm with no AC, what happens to them?"
- *"We've been doing it this way since the 60s."* → "And it's clearly working, you're still here. This just catches the ones who don't wait until morning."

**Voicemail (under 20 seconds)**
> "Hi Todd, Jordan with Returnline, I'm local in Conway. I work with HVAC shops on the calls that come in after hours. I'd love 10 minutes this week. My number is [PHONE], that's [PHONE]. Thanks."

## 2. Freyaldenhoven Heating and Cooling · (501) 764-9077
**Ask for:** John Freyaldenhoven. Scott, his brother, is co-owner.
**Fit:** ⭐ Strong. They advertise 24/7, and the voicemail asks for name, phone, address and what happened. They're a bigger shop that has run since 1970, so you may reach an office manager first. Treat them as a real contact, not someone in the way.

**Front desk**
> "Hi, this is Jordan with Returnline, I'm local here in Conway. Is John or Scott in?"
> *If not:* "No problem. Who handles your phones and scheduling? … Could I ask you a quick question instead?" Then run a short version of the owner script with them.

**John**
> "Hi John, this is Jordan with Returnline, local here in Conway. I know I'm calling out of the blue. Can I have 30 seconds, and then you tell me if it's worth talking?"
>
> "I called your line early Monday. Your voicemail asks for name, address and what happened, which is smart, because you're already trying to qualify the job before you call back. The thing is, a lot of people won't leave a long message like that. They just hang up and try the next company."
>
> "How do you catch the ones who hang up without leaving anything?"
>
> *(Let him answer.)*
>
> "What I set up texts those callers within a minute and asks the same questions your voicemail does: name, address, what's going on, how urgent. So you get the details from the people who'd never leave a voicemail. Could I show you in 10 minutes, Wednesday or Friday?"

**Likely pushback**
- *"We're big, we have staff."* → "Makes sense. After hours and on weekends, is someone picking up, or is it the voicemail?"
- *"People leave messages fine."* → "Good. Do you know how many hang up without leaving one? Your phone system usually shows missed calls with no voicemail. That's the number I'd look at."

**Voicemail**
> "Hi John, Jordan with Returnline in Conway, about your after-hours calls. I'd love 10 minutes this week. My number is [PHONE], that's [PHONE]."

## 5. Top Notch Heating & Air · (501) 545-5410
**Ask for:** Steven Long or Sara Long, the co-owners (local press also names Sara as the owner). Started in 2020.
**Fit:** Medium. They already have a phone menu (tech line, message or text), **and their system texts callers back in the morning.** They're already sold on texting, so pitch **speed and the qualifying questions**, not texting.

**Front desk**
> "Hi, this is Jordan with Returnline, local in Conway. Is Steven or Sara around?"

**Steven or Sara**
> "Hi, this is Jordan with Returnline, local here in Conway. I called your line early Monday and noticed your system texts people back in the morning. You're honestly ahead of most shops on that."
>
> "Can I ask what you use for those texts?"
>
> *(Let them answer. Real curiosity here.)*
>
> "When someone calls at 9pm with no AC, do you find some of them book someone else before the morning text goes out?"
>
> *(If yes:)* "What I do texts back within a minute, day or night, and asks what your office would ask: the problem, the address, how urgent, when they're free. So the job request is waiting when you wake up. Worth 10 minutes to compare side by side?"

**Likely pushback**
- *"We're happy with what we have."* → "Makes sense, you've clearly thought about it. Mind if I check back in a couple of months?" *(Then move on.)*
- *"What does yours do that ours doesn't?"* → "Two things: it replies within a minute instead of the next morning, and it has the conversation to get the job details. Easiest way to know is to compare on your phone for 10 minutes."

**Voicemail**
> "Hi Steven, Jordan with Returnline in Conway. I saw your morning text-back system, nice setup. I have an idea to make it instant. 10 minutes this week? [PHONE], that's [PHONE]."
