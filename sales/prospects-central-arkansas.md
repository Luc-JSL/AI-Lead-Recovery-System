# Central Arkansas HVAC Prospects (Conway + Little Rock Metro)

Researched 2026-09-28 for Returnline's in-person walk-in outreach. Conway is listed first (closest to founder). Only owner-operated / independent local shops are included — national franchises and multi-state chains (e.g. Airco Service, Dash Service, Middleton, Advantage Service Co., Paschal) were screened out.

**Research method note:** WebFetch (direct page fetching) was blocked network-wide by the environment's egress proxy for every HVAC company domain and every aggregator site tried (Yelp, Yellow Pages, downtobid.com, company sites, etc.) — every fetch attempt returned `EGRESS_BLOCKED`. All data below comes from WebSearch result snippets/summaries only, which quote or paraphrase listings on Google-indexed pages (company sites, BBB, Yelp, Birdeye, Facebook, local chambers). Facts that no search surfaced are left blank rather than guessed. Given the reliance on snippets rather than primary-source page reads, the founder should double check address/hours by phone or Google Maps before a walk-in visit.

## Notes / gaps
- **No shop physically headquartered in Cabot** turned up in search — the closest genuine local match is C & S Heat and Air, based in Austin, AR (a few miles from Cabot) and serving the Cabot area directly.
- **No dedicated Maumelle-only shop** turned up either — Central Arkansas Service Company is the one HVAC company actually headquartered in Maumelle; several other companies (Dash Service, Paschal, Ski Brothers, Kirby's) advertise service there but are based elsewhere and were mostly screened out as bigger multi-service operators.
- Rows with "Not found" for phone, address, hours, owner, or review count reflect an actual absence in the search results, not an oversight — per the task rules, nothing was guessed to fill those cells.
- A few companies could not be independently confirmed as small/owner-operated (Bert Black, Ski Brothers — both carry very high review counts, suggesting larger operations) — they are flagged above and the founder may want to deprioritize them if the pitch depends on being a small shop without a large call center.
