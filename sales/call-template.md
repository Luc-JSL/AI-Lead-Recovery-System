# Master call script (fill in and change as you like)

One script that works for every shop. Swap in the **[BRACKETS]**, and where there are options (A / B / C), pick whichever sounds most like you. Change any words you want. It only has to sound like **you**.

**Fill in before you dial:**
- **[OWNER]** = owner's first name (see `decision-makers.md`; if unknown, ask the front desk)
- **[SHOP]** = shop name
- **[WHAT I SAW]** = your test-call result, for example "it went to voicemail and said you'd call back in the morning"
- **[WHEN]** = when you called, for example "last night around 8" or "early Monday morning"
- **[DAY 1] / [DAY 2]** = two visit times, for example "Wednesday at 1" / "Friday at 12:30"

---

## If the front desk answers
> "Hi, this is Jordan with Returnline. I'm local here in [Conway]. Is [OWNER] around?"

- **Owner unknown:** "Hi, this is Jordan, I'm local. Who's the owner there? … Great, is [OWNER] in?"
- **"What's it about?"** "It's about the calls that come in after hours. I've got a quick question for [him/her]. It'll take 2 minutes."
- **Owner's out:** "No problem. When's the best time to catch [him/her]? And is there a better number?" *(Write it down.)*
- **They offer to help:** "Actually, maybe you're the right person. Who handles the phones when everyone's busy?" → go to Step 3 with them.

## Step 1: who, why, permission
Pick one:
- **A.** "Hey [OWNER], it's Jordan. I'm local here in [Conway]. Honest heads up, this is a cold call. Want to hear why I'm calling, and you tell me if it's worth your time?"
- **B.** "Hey [OWNER], Jordan here, I'm a local guy in [Conway]. I'll be quick. Can I tell you why I called, and you tell me if it's worth talking?"
- **C.** *(Your own version.)*

*(Wait for "sure" or "go ahead.")*

## Step 2: what you saw
Pick one:
- **A. You test-called them:** "I called your line [WHEN], and [WHAT I SAW]."
- **B. You haven't tested them yet:** "I called a handful of shops around Conway late Sunday night, and almost every one went to a voicemail that calls back the next morning."
- **C. They advertise 24/7:** "I saw you offer 24-hour service, and when I called [WHEN], [WHAT I SAW]." *(Say it kindly, never like "gotcha.")*

## Step 3: one question, then stop talking
Pick one:
- **A.** "So I'm curious, what happens to those callers overnight? Do most wait for you?"
- **B.** "How often do you call someone back and they've already booked someone else?"
- **C.** "When you're out on jobs, who's catching the phone?"
- **D.** "How are you handling that right now?"

**Stop. Count to 3 in your head. Let them talk.**

Then dig once:
- "How often does that happen?"
- "What's one of those jobs usually worth?"
- "Is it worse in summer?"

## Step 4: small ask
Pick one:
- **A. Nearby (Conway):** "Might be worth 10 minutes to show you what I do about that. Could I swing by [DAY 1] or [DAY 2]?"
- **B. Far (Little Rock area):** "Might be worth a quick 10-minute call to show you. Does [DAY 1] or [DAY 2] work?"

**If yes:** "Perfect. What's the best number to text you a reminder?" Then text Claude right away.

## If they ask "What is it?" before you've asked for the meeting
> "When a call goes unanswered, the caller gets a text from [SHOP] within a minute that asks what's wrong, the address and how urgent it is. The job request lands on your phone or your office's, so nobody calls the next company."

## If they say no
- "No worries. Mind if I ask, is it just not a problem for you, or is it timing?"
- **Timing:** "Fair. Could I check back in a couple of weeks?"
- **Real no:** "Appreciate you being straight with me. Have a good one." *(Mark them "not interested" in the pipeline.)*

## Quick answers (full list in `owner-questions.md`)
- **"How much?"** "$397 a month for my first three shops, no setup fee, and two weeks free first. But I'd rather show you before you decide anything."
- **"Is this AI?"** "An automated assistant handles the texting. It never quotes prices or diagnoses anything, and emergencies go straight to a person."
- **"How old are you?"** "I'm 20, and I'm local. That's why the first two weeks are free. You get to watch it work before you trust me."
- **"Send me an email."** "Sure, what's the best email? And can I check back Thursday?"
