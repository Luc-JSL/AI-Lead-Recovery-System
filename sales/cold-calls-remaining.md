# Cold calls: the other 16 shops

Same 4 beats as `cold-calls-conway.md`. **Know the beats, use your own words.**

```
1. WHO + WHY + PERMISSION   "Hey [name], it's Jordan, local in Conway. Honest heads up, this is a cold call.
                             Want to hear why I'm calling, and you tell me if it's worth it?"
2. WHAT I SAW (true)        see each shop below
3. ONE NEUTRAL QUESTION     see each shop below → then stop talking, count to 3
4. SMALL ASK                "Might be worth 10 minutes to show you. I could swing by Wednesday or Friday.
                             Would either work?"
```

## Best order: test first, then call
You haven't called these 16 after hours yet. **Tonight (7-9pm), make your test calls.** Then tomorrow you get to use the strongest beat 2 there is: *"I called your line last night around 8..."*

If you call one **before** testing it, use the **general beat 2** instead. It's true for every shop:
> "I called a handful of shops in Conway late Sunday night, and almost every one went to a voicemail that calls back the next morning."

Then ask their question below.

**Distance matters:** everything here is in the Little Rock area, so visits are a drive. For these shops, offer **a 10-minute phone or video call** first, or group visits by town on one afternoon (for example, North Little Rock on Friday).
