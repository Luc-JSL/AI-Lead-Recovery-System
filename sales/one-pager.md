## The problem

When your techs are on a job, or it's after hours, and a customer calls — what happens? For a lot of shops, it's voicemail. And a lot of customers won't leave a message — they'll just call the next shop instead. You've probably seen it happen.

Every missed call is a lead that might be gone for good.

---

## How it works — 3 steps

1. **A call comes in and nobody picks up.** Could be a busy line, after hours, or techs out on jobs.
2. **We text that customer back — fast.** They get a reply, we find out what's wrong, how urgent it is, and what time works for them.
3. **The job request goes to your office.** Your office confirms the actual appointment. You stay in charge of your schedule. We just make sure nobody slips through the cracks.

Anything that sounds like a gas leak, CO alarm, or other emergency gets an immediate safety message telling the customer to leave the house and call 911 or the gas company — and a real person gets notified right away. This never gets treated like a normal booking.
