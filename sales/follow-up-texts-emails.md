# Follow-Up Texts & Emails After a Walk-In

Use with whatever contact info you got at the door. If you only got a name and best time, call instead of texting.

---

## Day 2

**Text (under 300 characters):**

> Hey [Name], it's [Founder Name] with Returnline — stopped by [Shop] on [day]. Still happy to run a free 2-week trial on missed calls, no cost. Want me to start the missed-call count this week? [PHONE]

**Email (under 120 words):**

Subject: Quick follow-up from Returnline

Hi [Name],

Good talking with you (or with your team) at [Shop] the other day. Just following up on the free 2-week trial I mentioned. First we count your missed calls from your phone's call log, then we turn on a simple text-back so you can see what it catches. Your office still confirms every appointment. No cost, no contract either way.

Want me to get the baseline started this week?

[Founder Name]
Returnline
[PHONE] / [EMAIL]
