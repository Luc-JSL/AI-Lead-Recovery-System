# Owner questions: every answer, ready to say

Short, honest answers you can say out loud. **If you don't know something, say: "Good question. I'll find out and text you by tonight."** That answer builds trust. Guessing kills it.

Three rules:
1. **Never promise results.** We don't have any yet.
2. **Never say "AI" first.** Say "instant text-back."
3. **Ask a question back** whenever you can. Whoever asks the questions controls the conversation.

---

## 1. About you
**"Who are you? What is this?"**
> "I'm Jordan with Returnline. I'm local here in Conway. I help HVAC shops catch the calls they miss, so those customers don't call the next company."

**"How old are you?"**
> "I'm 20. I'm a student here, and I started this because local shops lose jobs every night to voicemail. That's also why I'm doing the first two weeks free: you don't have to trust me, you get to watch it work."

**"How long have you been doing this? Who else uses it?"**
> "Honestly, it's brand new. You'd be one of my first three shops. That's why the first three get a locked-in lower price, plus a guarantee."

**"Do you have references or results?"**
> "Not yet, and I won't make numbers up. That's what the trial is for. We count your missed calls first, and after two weeks you see exactly what it caught."

**"Are you a real company?"**
> "Returnline is my business, based here in Conway." *(Once the LLC is filed: "Returnline LLC, registered in Arkansas.")*

**"What if you disappear or go out of business?"**
> "Then you turn off one phone setting and everything's back to normal in a minute. Your number never changes. And I'll send you every customer conversation and contact, since they're yours."

## 2. How it works
**"How does it work?"**
> "When a call rings about 20 seconds and nobody picks up, it forwards to a line we set up. The caller hears a quick greeting, and within a minute they get a text from your shop's name asking what's going on. It gets their problem, address, how urgent it is and when they're free, then sends that to you or your office to confirm."

**"Do I have to change my number?"**
> "No. Same number, same phones. You turn on one setting called 'forward when unanswered.' It takes about 5 minutes."

**"If I answer the call, does it still text them?"**
> "No. It only kicks in when nobody picks up."

**"Whose name is on the texts?"**
> "Yours. The customer sees your shop's name, never mine."

**"What does the text say?"**
> Show the preview. "Hi, it's [Shop]. Sorry we missed your call! What's going on with your heating or AC?"

**"Does it answer the phone and talk?"**
> "Not right now. It texts. Most people answer a text within minutes. If someone would rather talk, they reply CALL and you get an alert to call them back."

**"What if they called from a landline?"**
> "A landline can't get texts, so you get an alert with their number to call them back. The lead still isn't lost."

**"What if they don't text back?"**
> "It sends one friendly reminder after 15 minutes and one the next morning, then it stops. It never spams people."

**"Does it book straight onto my calendar?"**
> "At the start, no, and that's on purpose. It collects the details and the time they want, and your office confirms. It never promises a time you can't keep. Later, if your calendar's always up to date, it can book open slots directly."

**"We use ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro or Jobber."**
> "Good. At the start, the job request comes to you by text or email and your office puts it in like any other job. Connecting it straight into your software is something we can look at later." *(Don't promise integrations.)*

**"What about existing customers calling about a bill?"**
> "It asks what they need. If it's not a new service request, it tells them the office will follow up and flags it for you."

**"What about spam and robocalls?"**
> "Spam doesn't text back, so it doesn't turn into anything. You only get alerts for real people."

**"Does it work nights, weekends and holidays?"**
> "24/7. Nights and weekends are when it helps the most."

**"What about Spanish?"**
> "It can reply in Spanish if the customer writes in Spanish." *(Before go-live, a native speaker checks the Spanish messages.)*

**"What if two people call at once?"**
> "Each one gets their own text and their own conversation, at the same time."

## 3. The "AI" questions
**"Is this AI?"**
> "Yes, an automated assistant handles the texting. It's set up with strict rules: it never quotes prices, never diagnoses anything, never promises a time. It just gets the details and hands them to you."

**"What if it says something dumb to my customer?"**
> "It's tested with dozens of tricky conversations before it ever goes live: angry customers, weird questions, people trying to trick it. You can read every conversation, and your office can jump in and take over anytime."

**"What if someone has a gas leak?"**
> "It doesn't try to help. It sends a set safety message (get out, call 911 or the gas company) and alerts your on-call person immediately. That part is locked. Nobody can turn it off."

**"Can I turn it off?"**
> "Anytime. Turn off the forwarding and it's off in a minute."

**"Can I read the conversations?"**
> "Every single one."

## 6. Legal and privacy
**"Is it legal to text my customers?"**
> "Yes, when it's set up right. They called you first, every text says they can reply STOP, and STOP is honored immediately. We also register your texting with the carriers, which is required now anyway, so your texts don't get flagged as spam."

**"Who sees my customers' information?"**
> "You and me, for setting it up and fixing problems. It's stored in the business software we run it on. I never sell or share it, and if you leave, it's yours."

**"Do I have to sign something?"**
> "For the free trial, I'll just text you a summary of what we agreed. Before any paid month there's a short, simple agreement."

## 8. "We already have something"
**"We have an answering service."**
> "Good. How's it working for you? … Do they book the job or just take a message? And what does it cost you a month?" *(Listen. If they're happy, thank them and move on. If they complain, that's your opening.)*

**"My office manager handles the phones."**
> "Makes sense. What happens after 5, or when she's already on another call?"

**"We use Podium or our software's texting."**
> "Does it text back missed calls automatically within a minute, and ask the qualifying questions? If it already does, you're ahead of most shops." *(Be honest. If it does, it's not a fit.)*

**"I could just set up an auto-reply myself."**
> "You could, and it's better than nothing. The difference is this one has the conversation: it gets the problem, address and urgency, alerts you, follows up, and handles emergencies. An auto-reply just says 'we'll call you back.'"

**"We're too busy already."**
> "That's a good problem. When you're booked up, are those extra callers worth catching for next week, or would they just be more pressure?" *(If they say more pressure, they're not a fit. Thank them.)*

**"It's slow season."**
> "Then now's the best time to set it up, so it's running before the next rush."

## 9. Stalls
**"Send me some info."**
> "Sure, what's the best email? … And can I check back Thursday to see what you think?" *(Always get a date. Then send the email.)*

**"I need to talk to my partner or wife."**
> "Of course. Would it help if I showed you both? It takes 10 minutes."

**"Let me think about it."**
> "Totally fair. What's the part you'd want to think over?" *(Usually there's one real concern. Answer it.)*

**"Not interested."**
> "No problem, thanks for your time." *(Stop. Mark it in the pipeline. Never push after a clear no.)*

## YOUR DECISIONS REQUIRED before your first meeting
1. **Is texting usage included in the price?** Recommendation: **yes, normal usage included.** The builder estimated about $12-26/mo per shop in texting and AI costs (unverified), so a $397 client still leaves a strong margin. "No surprise charges" is easier to sell.
2. **Your support promise.** Recommendation: **"Text me anytime. I reply within a few hours between 8am and 9pm."** Emergencies don't depend on you, because the system routes them straight to the shop's on-call person.
3. **File the LLC this week (~$45).** Recommended. It's needed for texting registration, and it lets you say "Returnline LLC."
