# Returnline — Pilot Agreement Outline

## What the pilot includes

- **Duration:** 2 weeks, free of charge.
- **Baseline period first:** before the text-back system goes live, we measure how many calls the shop is actually missing (from call logs / call-forwarding data), so there's a real "before" number.
- **Live period:** for the remainder of the pilot, missed calls trigger an automatic text-back, the AI asks about the issue, urgency, address, and a good time, and qualifying results get passed to the shop (booked appointment or a flagged lead for the office to call).
- **Reporting:** at the end of the pilot, the shop gets a plain summary — missed calls before vs. after, how many were recovered, and any jobs booked.
- **No obligation:** the shop is not billed and is not required to continue after the pilot.

## What we need from the shop

- **Call-forwarding access** (or permission to set up forwarding on missed/no-answer calls) so we can receive the pings when a call isn't picked up.
- **Business hours** (when the office is open, when techs are typically out).
- **Service area** (city/county/mile radius they'll actually run a job in), so the AI doesn't book jobs outside it.
- **Emergency contact** — a real phone number for a person who can be paged immediately if a safety-related call comes in (gas smell, CO alarm, etc.).
- **Booking method** — how they want jobs to land: a shared calendar, a text/email alert to the office, or direct entry into whatever field-service software they use (e.g. Jobber, Housecall Pro), if any.

## What happens at day 14

- We deliver the summary report (baseline vs. pilot numbers, leads recovered, jobs booked if any).
- The shop decides: continue at the agreed rate ($497/mo + $497 setup, or the $397/mo founding rate if applicable and still available), pause, or stop — no penalty either way.
- If continuing, we move to a simple month-to-month service agreement (separate document) covering billing, data handling, and cancellation terms.
- If the shop was a founding-rate client, they agree to a short case study and testimonial in exchange for the locked $397/mo rate.

## Texting-consent language (draft only — confirm wording with counsel and carrier/A2P requirements)

> By providing your phone number and participating in this pilot, [Shop Name] agrees that Returnline may send automated text messages on its behalf to customers who call [Shop Name] and are not immediately answered, for the purpose of following up on their service request. Customers may reply STOP at any time to opt out of further messages, and this will be honored immediately. [Shop Name] is responsible for ensuring it has the right to text its own customers under applicable law; Returnline will not send marketing or maintenance-reminder messages without separate, explicit consent from each customer.

## Other things to confirm with a lawyer before this becomes a real agreement

- Data handling / privacy language for customer information collected during the pilot.
- Liability limits, especially around missed or mishandled emergency calls.
- IP ownership of any case study content and how it can be used after the relationship ends.
- Termination and data-deletion terms if either side ends the pilot early.
