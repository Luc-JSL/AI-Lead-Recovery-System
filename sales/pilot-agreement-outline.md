# Returnline — Pilot (Free Trial) Agreement Outline

## What the free trial includes

- **Duration:** 2 weeks, free of charge.
- **Baseline count first:** the trial starts by counting the shop's missed calls from their phone's call log, so there's a real "before" number.
- **Live period:** for the rest of the trial, missed calls trigger an automatic text-back. The AI asks about the issue, urgency, address, and the customer's preferred time, then sends the job request to the shop's office. **The office confirms the actual appointment.** The shop stays in charge of its schedule; the AI does not book directly onto the calendar in phase 1.
- **Reporting:** at the end of the trial, the shop gets a plain summary: missed calls before vs. after, how many leads were recovered, and any appointments the office booked from them.
- **No obligation:** the shop is not billed and is not required to continue after the trial.

## What we need from the shop

- **Call-forwarding access** (or permission to set up forwarding on missed/no-answer calls) so we can receive the pings when a call isn't picked up.
- **Business hours** (when the office is open, when techs are typically out).
- **Service area** (city/county/mile radius they'll actually run a job in), so the AI doesn't book jobs outside it.
- **Emergency contact** — a real phone number for a person who can be paged immediately if a safety-related call comes in (gas smell, CO alarm, etc.).
- **Call log access** — a screenshot or export of the phone's call log so we can count missed calls for the baseline.
- **How job requests reach the office** — a text/email alert to the office, a shared calendar note, or an entry in whatever field-service software they use (e.g. Jobber, Housecall Pro), if any. The office confirms every appointment.

## What happens at day 14

- We deliver the summary report (baseline vs. trial numbers, leads recovered, appointments booked if any).
- The shop decides: continue, pause, or stop, with no penalty either way.
- **Pricing if the shop continues (no setup fee at either rate):**
  - **Founding clients (first 3):** $397/mo, locked for 12 months, in exchange for a case study and testimonial.
  - **Clients 4-10:** $497/mo (launch offer).
- **Term:** month to month, no long contract.
- If continuing, we move to a simple month-to-month service agreement (separate document) covering billing, data handling, cancellation terms, and the guarantee below.

## Guarantee terms (LAWYER REVIEW REQUIRED)

- **Promise:** "If we don't recover at least one real job in your first 30 days, that month is free."
- **Definition of "a real job":** a booked appointment from a new-customer lead that came through the Returnline system.
  - "New customer" means someone who is not already in the shop's customer list. [LAWYER REVIEW: confirm how this is checked and who decides.]
  - "Came through the system" means the lead started as a missed call that Returnline texted back (or another channel Returnline handles for the shop) and is recorded in Returnline's log.
  - "Booked appointment" means the shop's office confirmed a date/time with that customer. Because the office confirms appointments in phase 1, the shop agrees to tell Returnline when a lead it sent becomes a booked appointment. [LAWYER REVIEW: what happens if the shop doesn't report bookings.]
- **Points counsel needs to settle:**
  - When the 30 days start: day 1 of the free trial, or day 1 of paid service. [LAWYER REVIEW]
  - How "that month is free" works: no charge, a refund, or a credit toward the next month. [LAWYER REVIEW]
  - Whether the guarantee applies only to the first paid month or can repeat. [LAWYER REVIEW]
  - Whether a free month changes the start or end date of a founding client's 12-month $397 lock. [LAWYER REVIEW]

## Texting-consent language (draft only — confirm wording with counsel and carrier/A2P requirements)

> By providing your phone number and participating in this free trial, [Shop Name] agrees that Returnline may send automated text messages on its behalf to customers who call [Shop Name] and are not immediately answered, for the purpose of following up on their service request. Customers may reply STOP at any time to opt out of further messages, and this will be honored immediately. [Shop Name] is responsible for ensuring it has the right to text its own customers under applicable law; Returnline will not send marketing or maintenance-reminder messages without separate, explicit consent from each customer.
