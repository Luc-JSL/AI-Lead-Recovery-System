> "How do you catch the people who hang up without leaving a message?"

*(Stop. Count to 3.)*

> "Here's what I do. Your phone still rings first, same number. If nobody picks up, the caller gets a text from Freyaldenhoven within a minute. Then the whole job request lands with your office, and you confirm the time."

**5. Why it matters**
> "So you get the details from the people who'd never leave a voicemail, and they're talking to you instead of shopping around overnight. You already advertise 24/7. This makes sure every after-hours caller actually gets a response, without anybody answering the phone at 2am."
